# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful technical disagreement and evidence-based review. Participants must communicate without harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, or deliberate disruption.

Feedback should address behavior, code, design, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking a person. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

For conduct concerns, use GitHub's report/block tools or request a private contact channel from the maintainer without posting sensitive details publicly. Good-faith reports will be handled as privately as practical.
